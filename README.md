# Principality of Vessenmark

The Principality of Vessenmark is a self-declared micronation founded in 2019 by Aurel Dunmoreth, who serves as its Prince and head of state. Its capital and seat of government is Harrowell. Vessenmark is not recognized by any UN member state.

- Founded: 9 September 2019
- Founder and Prince: Aurel Dunmoreth
- Capital: Harrowell
- Motto: *Fidelis in minimis*

Website: https://ttkgas.github.io/vessenmark/
