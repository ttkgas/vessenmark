About Vessenmark

The Principality of Vessenmark is a self-declared micronation founded in 2019 by Aurel Dunmoreth, who serves as its Prince. Its capital is Harrowell. Vessenmark has no recognition from any UN member state and does not claim to be a recognized country. We describe ourselves as a small, long-term project in self-government, ceremony and friendship, carried out openly and within the laws of the country where our land lies.

Territory

Vessenmark's territory is a small privately held plot in Northern Europe, a little under one hectare of meadow running up to the edge of a pine wood. Harrowell is the name given to the plot's single timber cabin and the clearing in front of it, where the flag is raised and citizens gather. The Principality claims no land beyond this plot and makes no claim against any existing state.

Flag and motto

The flag has three horizontal bands of slate blue, white and slate blue, with a gold heron standing in the center of the white band. The heron was chosen because it waits patiently and moves only when it means to, which suits a nation of very modest size. The national motto is Fidelis in minimis, "faithful in small things."

Citizens

Vessenmark has around 140 registered citizens. Nearly all of them live elsewhere, in a dozen or so countries, and take part through correspondence, online meetings and the annual gathering. Citizenship is granted free of charge on application. It is a personal and ceremonial membership only: it carries no legal status, has no effect on anyone's actual nationality, and our certificates are not travel documents.

What we do

  • Hold a Founding Day gathering at Harrowell each September.
  • Publish council minutes and a quarterly bulletin for citizens.
  • Look after the meadow, including an ongoing tree-planting program along its northern edge.
  • Correspond with other micronation hobbyists who share an interest in constitutions, heraldry and civic design.
