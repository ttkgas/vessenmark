A Short History of Vessenmark

The Principality of Vessenmark was founded in 2019 by Aurel Dunmoreth, who has served as Prince since the founding. What began as a conversation among a few friends about how small communities govern themselves became, over a single year, a written charter, a flag and a small piece of ground to call home.

Founding

Aurel Dunmoreth had long been interested in civic history and in the hobby micronations that draft their own constitutions and keep their own registers. Early in 2019 he began writing a founding charter with a handful of correspondents. That autumn, on 9 September 2019, the declaration of the Principality was read aloud in the clearing of a small privately held plot in Northern Europe. The clearing and its cabin were named Harrowell, after an old iron harrow found rusting at the meadow's edge, and Harrowell was declared the capital in the same declaration.

Early years

The first citizens' assembly met online in 2020, when travel was difficult for everyone. It adopted the Constitution of Vessenmark, which set out the role of the Prince and created a council of five members. The first council was elected the following year, and the slate-blue and white flag with its gold heron was formally adopted at the same time.

Timeline

  • 2019: Founding charter drafted; Principality declared on 9 September by Aurel Dunmoreth; Harrowell named as capital.
  • 2020: Constitution adopted by the first citizens' assembly.
  • 2021: First council elections; national flag adopted.
  • 2022: First in-person Founding Day gathering held at Harrowell; the cabin roof repaired by volunteers.
  • 2023: The register of citizens passed 100 names.
  • 2024: Tree-planting program begun along the northern edge of the meadow.
  • 2025: Second full council term completed; quarterly bulletin launched.

Throughout its short history Vessenmark has remained what it set out to be: a self-declared micronation, unrecognized by any UN member state, that values patience, good record-keeping and the company of its citizens.
